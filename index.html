Bin 07 · Project

Google Shopping Setup

Route: Shopify's Google & YouTube channel → Google Merchant Center (the feed) → optional Google Ads (paid) and GA4 (tracking). Active setup ~30–60 min. First sync up to 24hrs. Google review after that takes several business days — products won't be live same-day.

BEFORE YOU START — CHECKLIST#PREP
  • Dedicated Google account for the businessNot a personal Gmail — cleaner for account recovery and permissions.
  • Shopify admin accessSales channels, Marketing, Discounts, Manage settings, Apps and channels.
  • Policy pages set up properlyRefund policy + Terms of Service under Settings → Policies (not standalone pages), linked in the footer using the "Policies" link type.
  • Visible contact info on the storefrontAt least one of: phone, email, address, or contact form.
  • Product data in shapeEvery product needs a description, a clean image (no watermarks/promo text), ideally a barcode (GTIN).
  • Shipping zones configured in Shopify for target countries
SETUP — STEP BY STEP#SETUP
1

Install & connect the channel

Shopify admin → Sales channels → Google & YouTube. Connect the dedicated Google account, allow access, fix anything on the setup requirements checklist.

2

Connect Merchant Center

Select or create an account → verify by phone (6-digit SMS code) → verify domain (usually automatic; if not, paste the HTML tag into theme.liquid below <head>).

3

Countries, languages & shipping

Countries/languages auto-sync from Shopify Markets by default. For shipping, choose automatic import (from your General shipping profile) or set rates manually in Merchant Center.

4

Let it sync, then clear errors

Check Merchant Center → Diagnostics as soon as products appear. Fix missing descriptions, images, GTIN/MPN, promo overlays, ALL-CAPS titles. Nothing shows until errors clear. "Pending" for a few days is normal.

5

Free listings go live

Once approved: Shopping tab, Google Search, Images, Lens — automatic, no extra step.

OPTIONAL — PAID (PERFORMANCE MAX)#ADS
  • Connect a Google Ads account (must be admin on it)
  • Set up a Performance Max campaign linked to Merchant Center — runs across Search, Shopping, Display, YouTube, Discover, Gmail
  • Set a daily budget your own margins support before switching this onDon't treat any online "recommended" figure as a rule.
  • Weekly: check search terms report, add negative keywords
TRACKING#REPORT
  • Merchant Center DiagnosticsCheck weekly while new — approvals, disapprovals, errors.
  • GA4Connect via same channel — on-site behaviour, conversion rate, revenue by channel.
  • Google Ads conversion trackingAuto-set up when Ads is connected — cost-per-conversion, ROAS.
⚑ Check first whether the business already has a Google account tied to Merchant Center or Ads from a previous setup, before creating a new one. Connecting fresh when the domain's already claimed elsewhere is the most common early snag ("domain claimed on a different account").
RESOURCES#LINKS
  • Shopify Help — Set up the Google & YouTube channelOfficial step-by-step + full troubleshooting section.
  • Shopify Help — Channel requirements
  • Shopify Help — Product disapprovals & warnings
  • Google Merchant Center Help
  • Google & YouTube app — Shopify App Store
  • Google Ads Help — Performance Max campaigns

Bin 08 · Project

Meta Ads (Facebook & Instagram)

Route: Shopify's Facebook & Instagram by Meta sales channel → one shared product catalog → feeds Facebook Shop, Instagram, product tags on organic posts, and Meta Ads Manager. Same catalog powers both the ads you pay for and the products you tag for free in posts/reels.

⚑ Since 26 Aug 2025, Meta is no longer a marketplace tax facilitator — we collect and remit tax on any Facebook/Instagram orders now, same as the website. Check Settings → Taxes and Duties before switching this on. Customers also now complete checkout on our actual Shopify store, not inside the Facebook/Instagram app.
BEFORE YOU START — CHECKLIST#PREP
  • Facebook account + a published Facebook Business Page for the business
  • Instagram professional/creator account linked to that PageNeeded if we want to tag products or run ads on Instagram too.
  • Business Portfolio (Meta Business Manager) that owns the PageIf a different portfolio owns the Page, it won't show up as connectable — worth confirming this before starting.
  • Ad account sitting in that same business portfolioShopify can create one during setup if we don't have it yet.
  • Shopify store not password-protected, and a valid sender email set in store settings
  • Tax settings configured for Facebook/Instagram ordersSee flag above.
SETUP — STEP BY STEP#SETUP
1

Install the channel

Shopify admin → Settings → Apps and sales channels → Shopify App Store → search "Facebook and Instagram by Meta" → Add channel.

2

Connect Meta account & assets

Click Connect account, sign into Facebook. Connect the business portfolio, Facebook Page, Instagram account, and ad account. Create any that don't exist yet.

3

Choose Maximum data sharing

Shopify offers Standard / Enhanced / Maximum. Choose Maximum — this turns on the Conversions API alongside the browser Pixel, which matters a lot for tracking accuracy (see below).

4

Let the catalog sync

Eligible products sync automatically into one shared catalog feeding Facebook Shop, Instagram, and ads. Initial review/approval can take up to 24 hours.

5

Confirm the Pixel is firing

In Meta Events Manager, browse the store, add something to cart, and check that PageView, AddToCart, and Purchase events show as received. Can take anywhere from 20 minutes to a few hours to show "Active" — don't panic if it's quiet at first.

TAGGING PRODUCTS IN POSTS#ORGANIC

Once the catalog's synced, products can be tagged directly in Instagram/Facebook posts, reels, and stories — same as any shoppable tag you've seen elsewhere. Tapping the tag shows price and links straight through to the product on the store. This works whether or not we're running paid ads on that post — it's free, and worth doing on every product post by default once it's live.

TRACKING RETURN ON AD SPEND#ROAS
  • Run Pixel + Conversions API together, not just PixelBrowser-only tracking misses an estimated 20–40% of conversions now, due to iOS privacy settings and ad blockers. CAPI sends the same purchase/add-to-cart events straight from Shopify's servers, so we still see them.
  • Check Event Match Quality in Meta Events Manager before spending real budgetA low score means Meta can't reliably match ad clicks to real store visitors — fix this before scaling spend.
  • Don't take Meta's reported ROAS as gospelMeta tends to over-attribute conversions to itself. Cross-check against Shopify's own numbers (Analytics → Reports → Sessions by traffic source, and actual order revenue) for a more honest read.
  • Track blended ROAS tooTotal store revenue ÷ total ad spend across every platform (Meta + Google, once that's running). Catches cases where Meta and Google are both claiming credit for the same sale.
CAMPAIGN STRUCTURE — STARTING POINT#SETUP
  • Start with a manual campaign, not Advantage+ ShoppingAdvantage+ needs 3+ proven creative winners before it has enough signal to optimise well — too early on day one.
  • Test a small number of creatives/audiences first, let data build, then scale what's working
  • Move to Advantage+ Shopping once we have real winners to feed it
REPORTING CADENCE#REPORT
  • Weekly: spend, purchases, cost per purchase, ROAS by campaign, best/worst creative
  • Monthly: blended ROAS across all platforms, contribution to total store revenue
CAMPAIGN STRATEGY — RETARGETING FIRST#PHASED

With a small, unproven budget, don't spread it across broad "find new customers" campaigns yet. Start with the audience that already knows the shop — it converts far better and proves ROAS fastest.

1

Phase 1 (weeks 1–2) — Retargeting

Audience: website visitors (last 30/90 days), email list uploaded as a Custom Audience, Facebook Page engagers/followers.
Budget: even £10–20/day is enough — warm audiences convert well on small spend.
Creative: dynamic product ads showing items people viewed but didn't buy, or a simple "come back — 10% off" offer.

2

Phase 2 (weeks 2–4) — Lookalike prospecting

Build a 1–3% Lookalike Audience from the existing customer list or website purchasers. Small daily budget, single objective (Purchases), let Meta's algorithm optimise. Only start this once Phase 1 has real data — don't split a tiny budget across both from day one.

IN-STORE ATTRIBUTION#TRICKY

Online ROAS is straightforward via the Pixel — in-store is the harder problem, since a customer can see an ad and just walk in.

  • Unique discount code or "show this ad" offer, redeemable in-store, so redemptions can be counted directly
  • Meta Offline Conversions API, or a manual upload of in-store transaction data matched against ad-exposed customers, if the POS can export it
  • Simplest option: pick one campaign, one offer, one time window, and compare in-store revenue for that period against the same period last month/last year
SET UP BEFORE SPENDING ANYTHING#CHECK
  • Meta Pixel installed and confirmed firing correctlyTest this first — bad tracking means no provable ROAS, whatever else is right.
  • Custom Audiences uploaded — email list, website visitors
  • One clear conversion event defined (Purchase), so the ROAS math is unambiguous
  • A tracking sheet — spend, revenue attributed, ROAS, updated weekly
CATALOG / DYNAMIC PRODUCT ADS — SETUP#CATALOG

This is what makes a Facebook post or ad automatically show catalogue photos, prices, and a working link straight to the product page — a Catalog Sales (dynamic product ads) campaign.

1

Commerce Manager catalog

business.facebook.com/commerce → create an "E-commerce" catalog. Since we're already connecting via Shopify's own Facebook & Instagram channel (setup steps above), the catalog syncs automatically — no need for a manual feed or upload.

2

Confirm Pixel/CAPI is tracking product views

This is what lets Meta match "viewed this product" to "show them this product in an ad." Already covered in the setup steps above — just worth re-checking here since dynamic ads depend on it.

3

Create the ad

Ads Manager → new campaign → objective Sales → at ad set level choose Catalog and select the catalog → pick the audience (retargeting, or Advantage+ catalog audience for prospecting) → carousel or collection format at ad level. Meta auto-generates the creative from the catalog.

⚑ Faster first test: skip the full dynamic setup initially and run a manual carousel ad instead — pick 3–10 products, upload photos, write titles/prices, link each card to its product page. No catalog dependency, launches same week. Move to full dynamic catalog ads once the approach is validated and it's time to scale.

Bin 10

Content Strategy & Platforms

What to actually post, and where to expand once Meta is working. Complements Bin 08 — this is the content side, that's the ads/tracking side.

WHAT TO POST — WHAT ACTUALLY DRIVES SALES#CONTENT
  • Social proof / UGCRepost customers using the gear (with permission, tag them). Outperforms polished product shots — reads as authentic, not an ad. A simple "tag us wearing it" discount incentive helps generate this.
  • Behind-the-scenes / staff pickse.g. "our top 3 picks for 5-a-side this month." Builds trust — positions the shop as expert, not just a seller.
  • Problem/solution posts"Struggling with X (blisters, grip, weight) → here's what fixes it." Ties the post directly to a purchase reason.
  • Urgency/scarcity — sparinglyLimited stock, end-of-season, "last sizes." Works occasionally; overuse erodes trust.
  • Local/community tie-insSponsoring a local team, posting club results, tagging local sports groups. Often the highest-converting content for a physical shop — hyper-relevant to nearby customers.
  • Short-form video demos15–30 sec: unboxing, "how to choose the right size," product in use. Gets more reach than static photos on every platform right now.
  • Consistency over production value3–4x/week of basic-but-real content beats one perfect post a month.
CONTENT MIX TARGET#RATIO
  • 40% product/offer posts
  • 30% educational / "how to choose" content
  • 20% community/UGC
  • 10% behind-the-scenes

Keeps the feed from reading as a constant sales pitch — which tends to hurt conversion, not help it.

PLATFORM EXPANSION#WHERE-NEXT
  • Instagram — first moveMeta-native, so the Facebook ads/catalog setup carries straight over. Reels get strong organic reach; sports/fitness content does well here.
  • TikTok — worth testing if the customer base skews youngerStrong for short demo/UGC content. Has its own ad platform with catalog ads similar to Meta's.
  • Google Business Profile / Google Shopping — high intentPeople searching "[product] [town]" are ready to buy now. Complements social, doesn't replace it — see Bin 07.
  • Pinterest — better fit for apparel/style-driven linesLess relevant for pure equipment.
⚑ Given the conservative-budget, prove-ROAS-first approach in Bin 08: stick to Meta (Facebook + Instagram together) until that's working and profitable, then expand to TikTok once there's a validated process worth scaling. Don't spread thin across every platform at once.
